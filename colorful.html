Journey
Five months, month by month

  

    
JulyInto a large legacy codebase
Took over the portal and the site platform. First security and access fixes, English admin screens for community sites, phone-friendly pages, the first version of the digital member card.

    
AugustSearch engines and speed
A database-driven sitemap (none since 2019), canonical URLs, GA4, redirects for old addresses. Image resizing and lazy loading. Reservations and albums on the site platform.

    
SeptemberA mobile app from zero
ASP.NET Core API over the existing database, an Expo app with Kakao/Apple/Google sign-in, store listings, and the first builds in testers' hands. Online ordering for restaurants on the portal.

    
OctoberRedesign, regions and AI
The app live on both stores. The portal's new design rolled out section by section; London, Ontario as its own region and domain; the AI assistant opened to everyone; Unicode migration of the database.

    
NovemberAI search and a new store
A full-page AI search with maps, voice and shareable answers; smsigns.ca, an online print store with instant pricing; and Seoul to Canada, a bilingual Korea ↔ Canada platform, started from scratch on .NET 10.

Mobile home page weight
4.1 MB → 0.85 MB on first load by lazy-loading below the fold. Listing page images 27.9 MB → 1.9 MB with an on-the-fly resizer.

1.4 GB

Unicode migration and 31 unused tables dropped. Every script: one transaction, a backup, a guard and a rollback.
